# "The House has an idea" — revive the suggested-market card

## What I found (verified)

The feature was never removed. Every piece is still in the codebase and wired up:

- The card component (`SuggestedMarketCard`) and its funnel hook are still imported and rendered by the center panel.
- The unified feed decides placement server-side and can return a `market_idea` item in the sequence.
- The generator job endpoint exists and a scheduled job pointing at it exists in the database.
- The card already reads the creator fee live from the contract and says: "Create it and earn X% of eligible trading fees generated by the market." So the 4.5% reward line is already the built behaviour, not something new.

Why nobody sees it: **the generator has produced exactly one idea, ever** (Aug 1) and nothing since.

The generator only considers wallets that recently *answered a House prediction*. In the database:

- 136 House prediction rows exist.
- Only 12 have ever been marked as answered, and the last one was **Jul 31**.
- Zero in the last 24 hours — which is the exact window the generator looks at.

So the candidate list is empty on every run, the job stores nothing, and the feed never has an idea to place. The card is alive but permanently starved.

## The fix

1. **Broaden the eligibility source.** Pick wallets from real recent decision history (actual YES/NO/PASS answers recorded in the belief/event history) instead of only the House-prediction answer column. The participation gate that decides whether a person has done enough to deserve a personalized idea stays exactly as it is — only the "who is worth considering" query changes.
2. **Diagnose the House-prediction write path** as a separate, smaller item: answers stopped being recorded on Jul 31, which also affects House accuracy. Report findings before changing anything there.
3. **Make the job observable.** Return counts of considered/stored/skipped-with-reason so a run that produces nothing tells us why (no candidates, model failure, all rejected as duplicates, etc.).
4. **Verify end-to-end**: trigger one generator run, confirm ideas are stored for eligible wallets, then confirm the feed places a `market_idea` item and the card renders with the live fee line.

## Should it appear in the market feed?

Yes — and it already is designed that way: the idea is inserted *into* the feed sequence as a card shaped exactly like a market card, at most once per session, with the same dock geometry (Create / Not for me where Yes / No sit). No change to that model is proposed. The reward sentence stays sourced from the contract's creator-fee value rather than a hardcoded 4.5%, so it can never drift from what a creator actually earns.

## Technical notes

- Change is confined to the wallet-selection query in the suggestion server module and the job's response payload.
- No schema change: `market_suggestions` and `market_suggestion_events` already have the right shape.
- The AI call path, validation, duplicate rejection and ranking are untouched.
